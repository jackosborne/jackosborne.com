<p>
      I’m a Scottish product designer and design leader based in Brooklyn, with more than fifteen years of experience. I work across early product exploration, consumer experiences, and design teams—moving between hands-on craft and leadership depending on what the work needs.
    </p>
<p>
      Most recently, I served as a <strong>Design Manager</strong> at <strong>Foursquare</strong>, where I helped lead, mentor, and grow the design team. Before that, I worked on Foursquare's <span class="mono">0→1</span> team, <strong>Labs</strong>, where I developed software that showcased how location could enhance a user's experience, making their everyday lives easier. Before joining the Labs team, I spent six years working on Foursquare's consumer applications, <strong>City Guide</strong> and <strong>Swarm</strong>.
    </p>

      At the beginning of my career, I spent five years at some of <strong>Scotland's most respected creative agencies</strong>. My work included a variety of clients, from <strong>national charities</strong> to some of <strong>the country's largest property and job markets</strong>.
