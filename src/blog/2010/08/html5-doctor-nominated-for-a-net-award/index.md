We're really pleased to annouce that the [HTML5 Doctor](http://html5doctor.com) has been listed under blog of the year in the latest .net magazine awards.

It's quite an achievement considering the blog is only a little over twelve months old. However, we find ourselves up against some tough competition; A list Apart, Abduzeedo, Blog.SpoonGraphics, Boagworld, CSS-Tricks, Smashing Magazine, Soh Tanaka's blog, ThinkVitamin and the Tuts+ Network.

If you haven't been able to guess by now, I'm using this blog post to canvas for votes. We would really appreciate it if you could take five minutes out of your day and head over to the [.net awards](http://www.thenetawards.com/) site and vote for the **HTML5 Doctor** under section five — blog of the year.
