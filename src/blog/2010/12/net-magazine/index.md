Last year I had my first piece of written work published, an article within .net magazine entitled [how to get your first job in web design](http://jackosborne.com/articles/jack-osborne-in-net-magazine/ "Get your first job in web design").

Getting something published within a magazine that I've had a subscription to, for many years, was very rewarding. So rewarding, that I decided to set myself a task — to get at least one piece of work published a year. However, the last twelve months have been incredibly hectic and at one point I thought that I was going to fail the task I had set. Thankfully, with one month to spare, I submitted another feature to .net, in the form of the website design off.

The brief was simple, create a small budget website for a local grocer/butcher/baker which showcased the quality of their produce. If you are interested in seeing my contribution then [click here](http://www.jackosborne.com/_/images/about/press/2010/NET209/NET209.pdf "Jack Osborne in .net Magazine").
